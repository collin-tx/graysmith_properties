Why investors choose Rachael

Five reasons investors work with me over a traditional agent.

01

I understand investor math

Cash-on-cash return, ARV, cap rate and debt service coverage are how I evaluate every deal I look at personally. When you're running numbers, I'll run them with you.

02

I've personally done what you're trying to do

Flip? Done it. House hack? Done it, still own it. Wholesale deal? Done it. Buy-and-hold rental? Doing it. I'm an investor who also happens to hold a license, and that combination is rarer than you'd think.

03

I know the Dallas market from an investment lens

I know which Dallas neighborhoods actually pencil out. Where rents support the numbers. Where appreciation has outpaced the rest of the metro. Where you can still find value before everyone else figures it out.

04

I have the connections you actually need

Reliable GCs who don't disappear after the deposit. Lenders who understand DSCR loans and investment financing. Property managers for buy-and-hold. These relationships take years to build. You get access to them on day one.

05

I won't talk you into a bad deal

An agent who gets paid on commission has a structural incentive to close deals. If the numbers don't work, I'll tell you. If the neighborhood doesn't support your exit strategy, I'll tell you. I'd rather lose a commission than cost you a bad investment.

Homeowners keeping the current house

New(ish) flippers

You want to try your first flip without betting the farm on it. I'll help you find a deal that pencils out and connect you with contractors who show up.
