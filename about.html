About Rachael

I'm not from Dallas. I just know it like I am.

I'm Rachael Gray Smith, a Dallas Realtor and Broker Associate with Compass, licensed in Texas since 2015.

I came to Dallas from West Texas almost 20 years ago without a roadmap. My first five years in real estate were spent in apartment locating, and that job had me living in just about every neighborhood in this city. You learn how a street feels on a Tuesday night that way, which tells you more than any listing photo.

Home for me now is Lake Highlands, in a house I chose for the schools, the neighborhood's trajectory and the next move I've already mapped out.

Experience

The experience behind the advice.

After my apartment locating years, I went to work with investors and started buying my own properties. Each one taught me how a house earns its money back, and how it can lose it.

  • The live-in flip. I bought a house, renovated it while living in it and sold it. Every decision was mine, and so was every surprise.
  • The duplex. I house-hacked it and still own it as a rental today.
  • The wholesale deal. I sourced it, renovated it with a GC and sold it for a profit.

Today both sides of that experience go into every move. You get help finding the neighborhood that fits your life, and I keep an eye on your investment whether you ask me to or not. I genuinely can't help it.

Approach

Why this approach works.

  1. The neighborhood comes first.

    Schools, commute, lot size and neighbors you'd actually invite over. I settle the neighborhood before you fall for a kitchen, because a renovation can fix almost anything except location.

  2. Every house gets the numbers.

    I check what the best homes on the street sell for, what buyers in that pocket pay for, and what's structural versus cosmetic. You'll know whether to renovate or buy finished before you make an offer.

  3. Every move is planned around the next one.

    My goal is for you to love the home you buy. When the day comes to sell it, it should sell with ease.
