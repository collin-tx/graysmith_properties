Before you start looking

Answer these before you scroll a single listing.

What does your Tuesday look like?

Gym, school drop-off, the office and the grocery run. A ten-minute difference twice a day adds up to a lot of patio evenings.

How much backyard do you actually want?

A pool, a play area and some breathing room from the neighbors all need lot depth. Lot size is the one thing a renovation can never add.

Renovate, or unpack and be done?

Some neighborhoods reward a buyer with vision. Others are full of homes that are already finished. I'll walk you through the math.

Good questions

Questions I hear a lot

Which Dallas neighborhoods do you work in?

I focus on East Dallas: Lakewood, Lake Highlands, Little Forest Hills, Casa Linda, White Rock, Old East Dallas, Lower Greenville and the M Streets. I also help buyers and sellers in North Dallas, Richardson and Plano.

Can I buy my next Dallas home before I sell my current one?

Yes. Most people use one of four approaches: sell first, buy first with a bridge loan or line of credit, write a contingent offer, or sell and lease back. The right one depends on your equity, your lender and your timeline. I'll map it out with you before you list.

Should I renovate or buy a move-in ready home?

It depends on the street. I check what the best finished homes nearby sell for, what buyers in that neighborhood pay for, and what's structural versus cosmetic. That tells us whether a renovation will earn its money back.

Should I keep my current house as a rental when I move?

Sometimes it's the smartest move you can make. I've done it myself with a duplex I still own. I'll run the rent, the costs and the sale numbers side by side so you can decide with real math.

Do you work with first-time real estate investors?

Yes. Most of my investor clients have day jobs. I help with first rentals, keeping a current home as a rental and first flips, and I connect you with lenders and contractors I trust.
